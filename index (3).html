About Sri Nehru Convent School

A Legacy of Discipline, Care & Academic Distinction

Established with the goal of bridging rural and urban educational disparities, Sri Nehru Convent School offers a holistic curriculum designed to prepare students for national entrance competitive tests while nurturing creative and athletic talents.

🎯 Goal-Oriented Coaching

Special morning and evening doubt sessions for Navodaya & Morarji aspirants.

🏡 Safe Residential Campus

Separate hostels for boys and girls with 24/7 security and hygienic dining.

🧪 Smart Science Labs
